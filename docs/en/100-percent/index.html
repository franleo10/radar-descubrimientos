GTA 6 100% checklist

Everything 100% asks for and how much you have left. Log your progress: it's saved in this browser, no sign-up.

What chapter are you on?

You're at 0% of the list

It's an average of each row. The real percentage is in the game, under Progress.

Before you start

  • 100% gives the gold trophy A Long Road. Guides estimate about 200 hours, versus 65 to 70 for the story alone.
  • You can see your progress on each item in the game, under Map › Progress › Collections. Some categories only show up once you find the first one.
  • Nothing on the list is missable: it's all still there after the credits. Still, some things are easier to do early: see the missables.
