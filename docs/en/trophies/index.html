GTA 6 trophies: the full list

All 55 GTA 6 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. Tick off the ones you have.

What you need to know

  • Platinum in a single playthrough. No difficulty levels and no online trophies. Guides rate it 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours.
  • No mission replay and no New Game+. Free roam continues after the story.
  • The only one that might be missable is Positive Mental Attitude, because of a chapter 1 meditation. See how to do it.
