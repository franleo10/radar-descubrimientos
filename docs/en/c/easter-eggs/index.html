GTA 6 easter eggs: every secret found so far

9 community discoveries, with the steps and the original video. Updated several times a day.

Easter eggs

Secrets in Jason's Burned House

If you go back to Jason's burned house, you'll find a stash with $5,000 and photos of his mom and his army days. Behind the staircase is his pet snake, which survived and laid eggs.

Easter eggsVice City

Vice City Multiverse: Playing as Tommy

A multiverse Easter egg that opens a portal to 2002 Vice City and lets you play at the Malibu Club. The rewards are Tommy's button-up shirt and the Malibu Zombie Bobber bike.

Easter eggsAmbrosia

Haunted Lighthouse Easter Egg and the Cursed Knife

On Little Hardin Island there is a haunted lighthouse with a ghost ship; following the clues leads to a unique green knife from a sunken shipwreck.

Easter eggs

50 easter eggs: Arthur Morgan's hat, lighthouse ghosts and more

A roundup of 50 secrets, such as Arthur Morgan's hat for $35 at a garage sale in Port Gellhorn and the ghosts of two lighthouse keepers on Little Hardin Island. It also mentions a secret bunker in Grassrivers and a mermaid in Wetman Bay.

Easter eggs

President Russo's Secret Bunkers

Explains how to open President Russo's secret bunkers using codes for the door, chest, and computer. The rewards are the 1922 pistol, the Cold War 556 rifle, a free tank, the Brainwasher pistol, and a permanent Focus bonus.

Easter eggsVice City

Hidden Secrets in Vice City with Rewards

Spanish-language video covering several secrets: Tommy Vercetti's shirt via a portal to Vice City, the Hellspawn shotgun in a hellish stage, hidden money, the Bugatti Thrax, a presidential bunker with a tank and weapons, and a boost to max health. Step-by-step instructions are not included in the text.

Easter eggs

Easter Eggs and References Compilation (Part 1)

Covers references to RDR2, GTA 5, Vice City, Silent Hill, Dexter, and Mario. Includes a ghost pirate ship, the Kraken, the Skunk Ape, a demonic ritual, and an alien UFO abduction.

Easter eggs

Pennywise-Style Clown in an Unfinished Building

In an apartment complex under construction, a balloon appears next to an out-of-service elevator. Inside, Jason finds balloons and a machete-wielding clown named Benny.

Easter eggsMount Kalaga

Psychedelic trip easter egg (Mushroom Trip)

A mysterious object transports Jason to a surreal world with floating platforms. Completing it grants a permanent stamina upgrade and a new tattoo.
