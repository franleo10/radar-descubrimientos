How to make money fast in GTA 6

Every way to make money, ranked from the best payout to the lowest, with the chapter it unlocks in.

What chapter are you on?

Amounts are the ones the first guides reported after launch and may change with updates.

  1. 01
    Big bank vaultsUp to $205,000 eachFrom: «That's Where the Money Is», chapter 4High risk

    They start at 4 stars. They pay in marked bills: you have to launder them with a fence before spending. There are 8.

    Per FandomWire · eGamers · Technerdiness
  2. 02
    Cars for Randy (Wish List)Around $30,000 to $35,000 per spotless carFrom: Mid chapter 3, after «Key Cloner»Medium risk

    16 or 18 cars, depending on the guide. Advanced trackers alert the police if you stop. Damage lowers the price.

    Per FandomWire · eGamers
  3. 03
    Pilot Pete deliveries (by plane)$12,000 the first one, $20,000 after thatFrom: Chapter 4Low risk

    Jason only. They're timed and every package has to make it.

    Per FandomWire · eGamers
  4. 04
    Small banksBetween $5,000 and $45,000From: «Credit Check», chapter 3Medium risk

    There are 14. Take the register and the safe, or it won't count toward 100%.

    Per FandomWire · eGamers
  5. 05
    Jewelry storesBetween $10,000 and $25,000From: Yasmani's jobs, chapter 3High risk

    Cut the alarm first. Jewelry is sold to a fence.

    Per FandomWire · eGamers · Technerdiness
  6. 06
    Payday Loans storesBetween $2,000 and $25,000From: «Credit Check», chapter 3Medium risk

    There are 7. The amount depends on the store.

    Per eGamers
  7. 07
    Smash 'n' Grab at pawn shopsBetween $1,000 and $25,000From: «Coffee Break», chapter 2Medium risk

    Staff may fight back.

    Per eGamers
  8. 08
    Gang operationsVariesFrom: From the startMedium risk

    There are 39 stash houses and they refill over time. Safes have the code nearby. What you take goes to a fence.

    Per FandomWire · eGamers · Technerdiness
  9. 09
    Robbing storesA few thousand dollarsFrom: From the startMedium risk

    Cut the alarm and wipe the security footage so they can't give out your description.

    Per Technerdiness · eGamers
  10. 10
    Cars for Moses (Port Gellhorn garage)Around $1,000 per carFrom: Chapter 2Low risk

    He won't take cars with a tracker. You have to wait about 2 minutes between sales and not steal near the garage.

  11. 11
    Pay 'n' SprayA couple thousand per carFrom: Chapter 3 according to two guides; another says from the startLow risk

    Check whether the car has a tracker before stealing it (parked, not from traffic).

    Per Technerdiness · FandomWire · eGamers
  12. 12
    Car trunksNot much: cash, weapons or stuff to sellFrom: From the startLow risk

    Almost no risk.

    Per Technerdiness

How not to lose what you made

  • What you carry is lost if you die or get arrested. One guide says the cash cap is $20,000: deposit often at an ATM or with BuckMe.
  • Each fence buys only one kind of item and pays less if it's damaged.
  • Scooter Bros. Premium costs around $12,500 and gives three free car deliveries per in-game day: according to one guide, it's worth it as soon as you can.
  • When chapter 1 ends you only keep what's in Family Savings.
