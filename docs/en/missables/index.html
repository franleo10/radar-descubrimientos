GTA 6 missables: what you lose as the story moves on

What disappears when you move to the next chapter, in order so you get it done in time. Minimal spoilers: pick your chapter.

What chapter are you on?

General rules

  • There is no mission replay. Save in several slots: there are 15 manual ones and 1 autosave. A good idea is to save at the start of each chapter.
  • Some fences close for good. Sell what you have before the mission that shuts them down.
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving. During that session you don't earn trophies and the game doesn't save. Save before using them.
  • 100% is never missable. Everything on the list is still available after the credits, in free roam.
  • Story trophies are never missable. They unlock by themselves as you progress. Jason and Lucía's relationship doesn't matter either.

Chapter 1

  • Meditation on Jason's balcony

    May be needed for the Positive Mental Attitude trophy. Guides disagree.

    After «Down to Earth», talk to Lucía in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and agree to meditate.

    Per KeenGamer · Bloxodes · PowerPyx
  • Magazines and books in Jason's house

    Three titles (Ashes of Glory, Lethal By Design and Body Copy) are only there. They count toward 100% (you need 30 of 34).

    Read them before starting «Tourist Trap».

    Per Mission Passed Guides · Game Checklists
  • The money you make in chapter 1

    When the chapter ends you only keep what's in Family Savings.

    Before wrapping up the chapter, spend or save what you have.

    Per Game Checklists · Mission Passed Guides
  • Shirley's errands (the rum and the scooter)

    They don't count toward trophies or 100%.

    If you want to do them, do them before «Tourist Trap».

    Per Bloxodes · Game Checklists

Chapter 2

  • Maravilla collection cards 1 and 2, at the Starlet Motel

    They disappear with the mission «Checking Out». Only one guide says so.

    Grab them before «Checking Out».

    Per Mission Passed Guides
  • Starlet Motel safehouse and the Delights Cabaret pool rival

    «Checking Out» removes them. The rival comes back when the club reopens.

    No rush for 100%: 5 of 7 rivals are enough.

    Per Game Checklists
  • End of chapter 2

    It requires finishing the chapter's other jobs and having at least $15,000.

    Save up that money first.

    Per Game Checklists

Chapter 3

  • «Race Against Yago» (hourglass mission)

    If you don't do it in time it can block Yago's missions and, according to one guide, the Strangers & Freaks trophy.

    Do it as soon as it shows up.

    Per Game Checklists · Bloxodes
