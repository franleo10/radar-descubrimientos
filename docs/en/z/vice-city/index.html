Secrets in Vice City

Every GTA 6 secret, easter egg, car, weapon and hidden place the community found in Vice City. 11 discoveries in this area, with the steps and the original video.

Secret locationsVice City

Secret Vice City Garage with Loot

A hidden garage north of Tequesta in Vice City, with a legendary silenced assault rifle with a red sight, a grenade launcher, and a body armor vest. There is also a safe to loot, and the location has no guards.

Easter eggsVice City

Two secret radio stations

Genie describes two hidden stations: The Vault Radio, with DJ mixes, and The Sniper of La Perle, a Haitian mixtape. Each one is unlocked by visiting specific places in Vice City.

VehiclesVice City

Six Secret Cars Inspired by Fast & Furious

The video shows six hidden cars in GTA 6 inspired by the Fast & Furious franchise, with their locations on the map marked by timestamps. The models include the Chevelle SS, Charger Daytona, Eclipse RS, Charger R/T, Challenger SRT8 and Skyline GT-R.

Easter eggsVice City

2002 Vice City Portal at the Malibu Club

Turning on the 12 electrical switches at LPL substations spread across the map and then entering the Malibu Club opens a portal to a retro version of Vice City, featuring Tommy Vercetti's Hawaiian shirt and his American Glory motorcycle.

OtherVice City

Where to buy chains and watches

States that Cuban chains can be bought at De'Luge Swimwear and gold watches at NOLA in Vice City, available from the start of the game.

OtherVice City

Ten Things to Do First in Chapter 3

Guide with several useful things from Chapter 3: a free sports car with a garage, the Snap Gun, legendary weapons, best vendors, higher bank capacity, and ways to make money. Includes missions and content that can be missed.

MoneyVice City

Selling High-Value Cars to Randy in Chapter 3

After the mission 'Steal The Zorrusso For Randy' you unlock the Key Cloner, which lets you steal expensive cars and sell them to Randy for easy money. The guide lists six Standout vehicles near his showroom in Vice City.

MoneyVice City

$10,000 and a Free McLaren

Get $10,000 from a safe, a suppressed pistol, and a free Progen Emerus from a container. The car can be registered at Pay 'N' Spray or sold.

MoneyVice City

Money Farm at the Guardia Brothers' Hideout

Method to earn over $50,000 by looting a gang hideout in Vice City without alerting the police, entering through the roof.

Tips and tricksVice City

100 Tips to Get Started

A guide with key tips: Chapter 1 resets your cash, so it's worth putting $5,000 into Family Savings and buying weapons and clothing beforehand; it includes a free Growler garage with a code.

VehiclesVice City

Classic Car Theft in Front of Police

Shows the theft of a classic Vice City car in front of the police, according to a Mythpat clip. It's a gameplay moment with no clear procedure.
